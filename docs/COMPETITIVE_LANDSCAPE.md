# Competitive Landscape

Research pass, October 2026. Answers one question: **who else is in PTK's space, and where
is the gap?**

Reference doc, not a build brief. Matters most at M3 (packaging) and launch.

---

## 1. PTK sits between two crowded categories

### Prison escape on Roblox — crowded, but all the same game

The prison category is full, and every major entry is the same shape: **modern-day
cops-and-robbers with team PvP.**

- **Jailbreak** — the genre's anchor. Police, prisoner and criminal teams; prisoners spawn in
  a cell with nothing and escape using a keycard pickpocketed or taken from an officer.
- **Riot City Prison Escape** — break out, rob the bank, collect 20+ vehicles and 15+ guns.
- **Valley Prison** — "realistic" prison roleplay with inmate, corrections, escapee and
  civilian roles.
- **Prison Life**, and **Breakout Prison** (formerly PRISONER LIFE).

**Benchmark worth keeping:** Breakout Prison has ~52 million visits, an 85% rating, and
roughly a 13-minute average playtime. That's a realistic comparable for a mid-tier prison
game.

**The gap:** every one of these is modern, gun-based, and roleplay-oriented. None is a
medieval loot-and-escape loop. A small *Medieval Prison RP* exists, but it's a roleplay
experience built around mining labour and rebel raids, not a progression loop.

### Extraction on Roblox — growing, but all shooters

Extraction is clearly trending, driven by ARC Raiders off-platform. On Roblox:

- **Escape from Roblox** — explicitly modelled on Tarkov, with traders and raid quests.
- **Extraction Raiders** — ARC-style robots, loot, extract.
- **The Armory** — TikTok-promoted, pitched directly as Roblox's answer to ARC Raiders.
- **GET OUT**, **Project Delta**, **Desert Storm Extraction**.

**The gap:** they are all **gun-based shooters**. Shooters skew older and are harder on
mobile. Off-platform, the fantasy-melee extraction lane is proven — Dark and Darker is the
reference — but **nobody has built a melee, fantasy, kid-readable extraction game on Roblox.**

---

## 2. PTK's position, stated plainly

> **The first melee fantasy extraction game on Roblox — a medieval Dark and Darker that a
> 12-year-old understands in 30 seconds, built for a phone.**

That's a real, defensible slot:

- **Not another prison game.** Jailbreak owns that and has for years. Competing on "prison"
  means competing on its terms.
- **Not another extraction shooter.** That lane is filling with ARC Raiders clones aimed at
  older, PC-leaning players.
- **Melee is the mobile advantage.** Tap-to-swing with forward magnetism is far kinder on a
  phone than aiming a gun. Blueprint §11 already designed for this.

## 3. What this changes

### Title and search keywords

"Prison" is the most contested keyword in the category. Searching it surfaces Jailbreak and
four cops-and-robbers games first.

**Keep `PRISONER TO KING`** — the transformation in the title is the hook, and it's distinct
from every competitor's name. But don't lean on "prison" in the description or update
suffixes. Lean on the words where competition is thin:

- **Use:** castle, steal, loot, escape, dungeon, knight, king, raid, legendary
- **Avoid as primary terms:** prison, jail, cops, inmate

Blueprint §6's "Escape the King's Dungeon" alternative is now the stronger *secondary*
phrasing, for the same reason.

### PvE is a differentiator and a cost

Every prison competitor is team PvP, which gives them social interaction for free. PTK is
PvE, which makes it kinder to young players and a clearer loop — but it means **social has to
be designed in deliberately.** This is the competitive version of the co-play finding in
`MARKET_AND_REVENUE.md`, and it reinforces putting the co-play hook in M2.

### The key-locked extraction route is validated

ARC Raiders' extraction includes "raider hatches" — safer exits that require a key. That is
the exact mechanic in BLUEPRINT §59: *"1 extraction route plus 1 unlockable shortcut."*

**This was scoped for the MVP and never built.** M1 has one sewer exit. A locked shortcut is a
cheap, proven way to make a second run feel different from the first — it belongs in M3's
Greenvale scope at the latest, and could reasonably slot into M2 if a run-2 reason is still
missing after Bronn.

### Weekly exclusives are the proven return trigger

Grow a Garden boosts retention with weekly exclusive items players must be online to claim.
That's the cleanest model for PTK's M3 live-ops layer — a rotating weekly weapon in the
vault, say — and it fits the 28-day retention window better than daily login calendars.

## 4. Threat to watch

The extraction-shooter wave is fast-moving. If a well-funded studio ships a **melee** fantasy
extraction game on Roblox before PTK launches, the positioning above collapses into "the
second one." That isn't a reason to rush a weak launch — the new-game boost argument still
holds — but it is a reason not to let M2 and M3 sprawl.

**Check every few weeks:** search Roblox for "dungeon extraction", "medieval loot escape",
"knight extraction". If something appears, compare its loop to PTK's before reacting.
